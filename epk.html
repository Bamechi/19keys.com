01 — The Bio

Global Futurist.
Cultural Thought Leader.

19Keys is a global futurist, cultural thought leader, entrepreneur, AI educator, and host of the award-winning, Top 1% podcast High Level Conversations. His work sits at the intersection of culture, technology, ownership, wellness, spiritual intelligence, and self-mastery — translating complex ideas about the future into clear frameworks that help people think higher, build smarter, and move with purpose.

Through High Level Conversations, 19Keys has helped generate over 300 million views across long-form conversations, clips, and community-driven media. Through Ziion.io, a future-focused learning and community platform with over 30,000 members, he helps audiences access high-level education, masterclasses, AI tools, and collaborative spaces built for implementation.

19Keys has led multiple AI hackathons and has been teaching future technology, digital ownership, and AI-centered frameworks for more than seven years. He has spoken and activated audiences in over 17 cities globally — from Toronto to London to Nigeria and beyond — bringing future-facing insight to leaders, entrepreneurs, creators, and communities seeking practical strategy, higher thinking, and real-world transformation.
